AgriTech Investment Analysis: XAG (Agricultural Drone Company)

A full investment evaluation of XAG, combining financial modelling, competitive benchmarking, and ESG impact analysis to support investment decision-making.

  • Investment Thesis: Conducted a full investment evaluation of XAG, focusing on its role and growth potential within the precision agriculture market.
  • Market Analysis: Analyzed industry growth trends and estimated market potential using publicly available data and industry reports, identifying key demand drivers and adoption barriers.
  • Financial Modelling: Built a 5-year financial projection model to assess revenue growth, profitability, and scalability under multiple operating scenarios.
  • Competitive Benchmarking: Performed competitive benchmarking against DJI, identifying strategic positioning, product differentiation, and defensible market segments.
  • ESG Assessment: Evaluated ESG impact, including environmental efficiency gains and broader implications for sustainable agriculture investment.
  • Deliverable: Synthesized findings into an investment-style memo presenting the recommendation and supporting analysis to inform decision-making.
